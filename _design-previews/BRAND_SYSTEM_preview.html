Dark-mode long-form comparison — option A vs option 2

Same content, same hero + section title + body paragraphs — only the body text color differs. Read both panels at length. Does sage-on-slate strain your eyes compared to cream-on-slate? If no, option A wins on metaphor purity + palette simplicity. If yes, option 2 (cream body) earns its place.
OPTION A — full inversion · body in SAGE · 12.53:1
Chalk World.

A shared canvas for you and the AI.

A fresh take on visual collaboration with AI.

Why “chalk”

Chalk is one of the oldest drawing mediums there is. Primitive — cave paintings, slate, a kid's sidewalk drawing. It represents the fundamentals of visual communication. And the AI has now been handed a piece of chalk for the first time.

Chalk is ephemeral by nature, not just erasable. That's a feature, not a flaw. Chalk is for thinking in the moment; the persistence of what the thinking produced lives elsewhere — in the save format, the state, the record underneath the canvas.

Body text: sage #DDE3D6 on slate #1a211d. One color family across both brand moments and body. Cream is dropped.
